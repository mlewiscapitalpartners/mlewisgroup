Who We Are

Strategic Advisory. Private Capital. Long-Term Partnership.

M. LEWIS GROUP is a Zambia-headquartered institutional platform spanning investment, advisory, venture building, private capital and research. The platform is focused on building businesses into growth-ready enterprises, financing their expansion, and scaling their impact across Africa's productive economy.

We combine venture-building, strategic advisory, private capital, and operating partnerships to help companies strengthen their foundations, become growth-ready, and transition from promising local businesses into scalable regional platforms.

Our focus extends beyond investing in companies. We identify businesses positioned at critical points in Africa's value chains and work alongside them to strengthen the systems required for growth — connecting capital, technology, production, infrastructure, logistics, and markets to build more competitive and scalable enterprises.

The Important Distinction

The M. LEWIS GROUP is relationship- and opportunity-led rather than product-led. A client might initially come for strategic advice but subsequently need capital. An investment opportunity might lead to advisory work. A venture the platform helps build might eventually become an investment or M&A client.

This integrated, multi-disciplinary approach allows us to serve as a long-term partner across the entire business lifecycle.

OUR STORY

Building, Financing and Scaling Africa's Next Generation of Regional Businesses

The story of M. LEWIS GROUP — why we exist, what we believe about Africa's next economy, and the platform we are building.

Why We Exist

Africa does not only need more capital. It needs more productive businesses capable of absorbing it.

M. LEWIS GROUP was founded on a straightforward conviction: the next phase of Africa's economic growth will not be driven by capital alone. It will be driven by businesses capable of deploying technology, creating jobs, developing infrastructure, adding value to resources, and connecting markets.

Too often, capital sits waiting for the right business. Too often, promising businesses wait for the right capital. The gap between the two is rarely a shortage of money. It is a shortage of the businesses, systems and support structures that allow capital to be deployed productively and at scale.

M. LEWIS GROUP was built to close that gap — by building, financing and scaling businesses that strengthen Africa's productive economy.

"Africa's next economy will not simply be consumed. It will be built."

Where We're Going

Our Long-Term Ambition

M. LEWIS GROUP is being built as a long-term African strategic capital platform. Not the largest firm on the continent — the most effective one at identifying, building, financing and scaling productive African businesses.

Over time, the platform will evolve from deploying balance-sheet capital into managing dedicated investment structures — venture and growth capital funds, private credit vehicles, sector-specific platforms and co-investment vehicles — allowing the Group to move from individual transactions toward a repeatable capital deployment model.

By the end of our first five years, we aim to have:

→ A strong advisory practice

→ A diversified portfolio of operating businesses

→ Strategic investments across selected sectors

→ A network of entrepreneurs and institutional partners

→ Repeatable investment and value-creation processes

→ Regional operating capabilities

→ Dedicated capital vehicles

→ A portfolio of interconnected businesses

The Long-Term Thesis

Africa's next generation of significant companies will not be built by companies operating in isolation. They will emerge from connected systems.

→ Energy will enable industry.

→ Finance will enable enterprise.

→ Technology will increase productivity.

→ Logistics will connect production to markets.

→ Manufacturing will create value from resources.

→ Regional integration will provide scale.

→ Digital infrastructure will connect businesses and customers.

M. LEWIS GROUP intends to operate at the intersection of these systems — identifying the businesses capable of becoming important nodes within them and providing the strategy, capital, relationships and operating support required to scale them.

M. Lewis Consulting & Research is an independent financial consulting, market entry advisory, and equity research firm based in Zambia, providing rigorous, research-driven insights to businesses, investors, and institutions navigating opportunities across Zambia and African markets.

We combine financial expertise, market intelligence, and independent research to help clients understand markets, evaluate opportunities, solve complex business questions, and make informed financial and strategic decisions.

Our approach is grounded in evidence, analytical rigor, local market understanding, and practical commercial relevance. Our role is to turn complex financial and market information into clear, decision-useful insight.

Independent insight for better financial and strategic decisions.

Our Research Philosophy

Every engagement and research product is built around a set of core principles.

Independent

Maintain analytical independence and clearly disclose relevant conflicts or limitations.

Rigorous

Use appropriate financial, economic, commercial, and research methodologies.

Evidence-Based

Ground conclusions in reliable information and distinguish verified information from assumptions and estimates.

Contextual

Interpret financial and market information within the relevant African economic and commercial environment.

Practical

Focus on insights that can support real decisions.

Transparent

Make assumptions, methodologies, limitations, and uncertainties clear.

Our Pricing Model

Three Engagement Models

Basiwel uses three principal engagement models. The right one depends on the venture, the founder's objectives, and Basiwel's expected contribution.

📋

1. BUILD — Fee-Based

The client pays Basiwel for defined venture-building work.

Best for

Founders who want to retain maximum ownership.

🤝

2. BUILD + PARTNER — Fee + Equity

The client contributes a reduced cash fee and Basiwel participates through an agreed equity position.

Best for

Promising early-stage ventures where Basiwel expects to contribute substantial execution value.

🚀

3. VENTURE PARTNERSHIP — Equity / Investment

Basiwel becomes a deeper strategic and execution partner in selected ventures.

Best for

High-potential opportunities with significant long-term value creation potential.
